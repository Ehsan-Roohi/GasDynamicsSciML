# Revision evidence summary

All values below are generated by `gasdynbench --output results/revision` on the CPU environment recorded in `results/revision/environment.json`.

## Findings that materially changed the paper

1. **Rayleigh target correction.** Static `T/T*` peaks at `M=1/sqrt(gamma)`, so a subsonic/supersonic split at `M=1` is ambiguous. The revised inverse uses stagnation `T0/T0*`.
2. **Fanno near-sonic failure.** The raw-output ablation has near-sonic MAE `3.232e-3` and produces negative friction-length values for 96.9% of the local audit. The structured positive model has MAE `1.226e-8` and zero invalid outputs.
3. **Multivalued oblique inverse.** A naive single-output MLP has branch-distance MAE `0.4246 rad`; branch outputs reduce this to `5.076e-4 rad` and preserve ordering for every blind state.
4. **Classical baseline.** PCHIP dominates the MLP in accuracy and time for covered one-dimensional tables. Two-dimensional interpolation has incomplete convex-hull coverage. This is a benchmark result, not an inconvenience to hide.
5. **Measured cost.** For 5,000 queries, MLP/root speed-ups are 196x (Rayleigh), 389x (Fanno), 96x (oblique), 1,057x (nozzle), and 102x (shock tube). Interpolation is faster than the MLP in every case.

## Machine-readable files

- `primary_metrics.csv`: blind relative L2/Linf, MAE, RMSE, and maximum absolute error.
- `baseline_comparison.csv`: neural/interpolation accuracy plus interpolation coverage.
- `ablations.csv`: raw-versus-structured Fanno and naive-versus-branch oblique results.
- `near_singular_metrics.csv`: four local distance bins for every problem.
- `range_generalization.csv`: interior-box training versus omitted edge-band testing.
- `physical_diagnostics.csv`: entropy, residual, endpoint, and validity checks.
- `seed_uncertainty*.csv`: three-seed runs and aggregate mean/standard deviation.
- `training_size_scaling.csv`: 200/500/900 samples over three seeds.
- `timing.csv`: warm-up, median, and IQR wall-clock evidence.

## Interpretation boundary

The reported blind grids contain unseen states and combinations inside declared domains. They do not establish unrestricted extrapolation. Hard output transforms enforce selected admissibility conditions but do not prove all conservation identities. Offline data generation and training are not included in inference timing.
